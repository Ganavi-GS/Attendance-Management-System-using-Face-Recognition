How to Mark Attendance

  1. Click "Mark Attendance" from the menu above
  2. Click "Start Face Recognition" button
  3. Camera will open - position your face clearly
  4. System recognizes your face automatically
  5. Attendance marked! You'll see a success message

Important Notes

  • ✅ You can mark attendance once per day
  • 💡 Ensure good lighting for better recognition
  • 👤 Face the camera directly
  • 🚫 Remove glasses or masks if face is not recognized
  • ⏰ Mark attendance during class hours
